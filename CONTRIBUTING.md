Every commit you submit for inclusion must include a `Signed-off-by` trailer.
**By submitting a signed-off commit, you certify the Developer Certificate of
Origin (DCO) 1.1 and agree to the additional contributor license grant below.**
Read both before signing off.

## Sign off your commits

Read the unmodified [Developer Certificate of Origin 1.1](https://developercertificate.org/).
It certifies your authority to submit the work under the applicable open source
license and acknowledges that the contribution and sign-off details become a
public record retained indefinitely.

Add your sign-off when creating a commit:

```sh
git commit --signoff
```

Git adds a trailer using the name and email address in your Git configuration:

```text
Signed-off-by: Your Name <you@example.com>
```

Use your own name and email address. Each contributor must supply their own
sign-off; do not add a sign-off on someone else's behalf without their permission.

To add your sign-off to your most recent commit:

```sh
git commit --amend --no-edit --signoff
```
